Overview

What is being built

A marketing and booking website for an NYC chauffeur service — built to generate calls, quote requests and online bookings, not just to look good.

The site is roughly eighteen public pages, an online booking and quote system, an admin dashboard for managing incoming requests, and the email pipeline that connects them. It runs on a single server that we manage.

Three things are needed to finish it. The first is the one that holds everything else up.

Information from you

Real business details, confirmation of which services you genuinely offer, your coverage area, and the fixed airport rate card. Pages cannot be written against placeholders.

Photography

Photographs of your actual vehicles and chauffeurs. The longest lead time on the project — worth arranging this week.

Accounts and hosting

A server, a mailbox and a handful of free service accounts, listed with costs further down. All registered in your name.

Architecture

How it is put together

One server runs everything. Cloudflare sits in front of it, absorbing traffic spikes and serving cached pages close to the visitor.

Cloudflare DNS · CDN · HTTPS · firewall · bot protection — free plan
Nginx Routes requests, terminates TLS, serves images and static files
Next.js — port 3000 The public website: every page a customer sees
Express API — port 4000 Booking and quote submissions, email dispatch, admin dashboard
MySQL — local only Bookings, quote requests, customers — not reachable from the internet

Correction · cPanel is not included

A Namecheap VPS ships as a bare Linux server. cPanel is a separate paid licence, roughly $15–45 per month depending on tier, and it is not needed here. MySQL installs directly on the server in about ten minutes and costs nothing. If you want a visual way to browse the database, we will set up a free admin tool over a secure connection instead.

Core feature

Booking and quote system

This is the part of the site that earns its keep. A request submitted by a customer travels straight to your inbox and into the dashboard.

What the customer fills in

Pickup and drop-off with address autocomplete, date, time, passengers, bags, vehicle class, service type, name, phone, email, and special instructions. Airport bookings add airline, flight number, and arrival or departure.

What happens next

The customer sees a confirmation page and receives a confirmation email. You receive an immediate notification with every detail. The request appears in the dashboard marked New.

Managing requests

The dashboard lists bookings and quotes with pickup, destination, date, vehicle and customer contact details. Each moves through New, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled or Pending.

On email delivery

Booking confirmations are sent through a dedicated delivery service rather than the mailbox, so they reliably reach the inbox instead of the spam folder. A missed confirmation is a lost booking. Your mailbox stays for human correspondence.

Findability

Search and local presence

  • A unique page title and description for every page — no duplicated titles
  • Clean URLs such as /jfk-airport-car-service, with one clear heading per page
  • Structured data describing the business, its service area and its services, validated before launch
  • XML sitemap and robots file, submitted to Google Search Console
  • Website details kept identical to the Google Business Profile — name, phone, hours, services
  • Genuine local content about the areas you actually serve, written naturally rather than stuffed with keywords

No agency can guarantee a number one ranking, and we will not claim one. What we can guarantee is a fast, correctly structured, genuinely useful site — which is what local ranking is built on.

Scope

Assumptions and exclusions

  • No online payment. Customers request a booking; you confirm and collect payment as you do today. Card payment can be added later as a separate piece of work.
  • No live GPS vehicle tracking. The tracking page shows booking status. Real-time vehicle positions require hardware or a dispatch system integration.
  • No dispatch software integration unless you tell us which system you use.
  • Copy is drafted by us and approved by you. Facts about the business must come from you.
  • Photography is commissioned by you. We will provide a shot list and direction.
  • Accounts are owned by you. Domain, server, Google and email accounts stay in your name, so you retain control of the business's assets.
